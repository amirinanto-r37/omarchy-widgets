# hootling

A pixel owl virtual pet in your Omarchy theme. Feed, play with, clean, bathe and cure it; it hatches
as a chick, grows into an owl and earns a scholar's cap with good care. Caring earns coins and xp:
level up, keep a daily streak, meet daily goals and collect badges, and spend coins on treats,
outfits, homes, toys, decor and eggs of animal friends that keep it company on the branch.

It runs no Linux commands: everything comes from Android (clock, battery, media).
